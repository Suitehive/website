**What you'll learn:** how the first organization and admin are created, how the setup wizard works, and
what happens the first time you sign in.

## How the first admin is created

On a server install, `suitehive install` creates your organization, your domain and the first admin for you.
It prints the admin email and, if you didn't choose one with `--admin-password`, a generated password.
That password is shown once and is never written to disk.

If the installer can't finish this step (for example, Auth wasn't ready yet), it tells you to open
`https://suite.example.com/setup` and finish in the browser instead.

In local mode there is nothing to set up: a demo organization with three users already exists.
See [Quick start](/docs/getting-started/quick-start/).

## The setup wizard

The wizard at `/setup` is only available until the first organization exists. It has five steps.

<Steps>

1. **Organization.** Enter the organization name. The short name (lowercase letters, numbers and dashes)
   is filled in from it; you can change it.

2. **Your admin account.** Enter your name, your email (the part before `@` and your domain, for example
   `ada` and `example.com`) and a password of at least 12 characters. A strength meter checks it as you type.
   Select **Create workspace**.

3. **Domain.** The wizard shows a TXT record that proves you own the domain, with a copy button. Add it at
   your DNS provider. The wizard checks every 10 seconds and shows the result live. You can select
   **Verify later**.

4. **Mail DNS.** The wizard lists the MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC and autodiscovery records with a live check
   for each one. Select **Check again** after you add them, or **I'll do this later**.

5. **Done.** Links take you to Mail, Calendar and the Admin console.

</Steps>

You can finish the DNS steps any time later in **Admin → Domains**. See [DNS setup](/docs/getting-started/dns/).

## Sign in

Open `https://suite.example.com` (or `http://localhost:8080` in local mode). The address opens Mail, which
sends you to the sign-in page.

<Steps>

1. Enter your email and select **Continue**. If your organization uses an external sign-in provider, you also
   see a button such as **Continue with Google**.

2. Enter your password and select **Sign in**. If Caps Lock is on, the page tells you.

3. If you have 2-step verification, confirm with your passkey or the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
   Lost your phone? Select **Use a recovery code**.

</Steps>

After too many wrong passwords, the page asks you to wait and shows a countdown. Error messages never say
whether the email or the password was wrong.

Forgot your password? Select **Forgot password?** to get a reset link by email. Resetting signs you out
on every other device.

## Turn on 2-step verification

New organizations require 2-step verification for admins. The first time you sign in as an admin, you're
asked to set it up before you can continue.

<Steps>

1. Go to **Account → Security**.

2. Add a passkey, or select **Set up** next to **Authenticator app** and scan the QR code.

3. Save your recovery codes. Use **Download** or **Copy**, then confirm **I saved my recovery codes**.
   You can't continue without confirming.

</Steps>

The demo organization in local mode does not require 2-step verification.

<Aside type="tip" title="Mail and calendar apps">
Desktop and phone apps don't use your account password. Create an app password for them. See
[Connecting apps](/docs/user-guide/connecting-apps/).
</Aside>
