**What you'll learn:** how to find a time that works for everyone and how to add a room to an event.

## Find a time

<Steps>

1. Add guests to the event, then open the **Find a time** tab in the editor.

2. A day grid shows one column per person ("You" first) with their busy times. Your proposed time is a band
   across the columns. Drag it, or focus it and use `↑` and `↓`, to try other times. Use the arrows above the
   grid to change the day.

3. Under **Suggested times**, select a chip to move the event to that slot. Suggestions come from the next
   7 days within working hours. A chip that only works because some optional guests are busy says so.

</Steps>

Find a time only works for timed events. If the event is all day, turn off **All day** first.

People outside your organization show **Outside your organization: no availability**. Suitehive can only see
the free/busy times of people on your server.

Free/busy only shows when someone is busy, never what they're doing. Events marked **Show as: Free** don't
count as busy.

## Rooms

Rooms are calendars for meeting rooms and equipment. They appear under **Rooms** in the sidebar. The local trial comes with demo rooms.

### Add a room to an event

<Steps>

1. Open the **Rooms** tab in the editor.

2. Filter by **Capacity** (for example "6+ people") and **Features** (Video, Whiteboard, Projector, Phone,
   Step-free).

3. Each room shows its capacity, floor, building and features. A room that's taken shows **Busy** with the
   conflicting time. Select **Add** on a free room.

</Steps>

You can also pick a room from **Add room** in quick create.

### Find a room from the sidebar

Select **Find a room** in the sidebar and search by name, floor or building. **Show** adds the room's calendar
to your view; **Book** opens a new event with that room already added.

### How rooms answer

Each room has a booking policy set by your admin:

| Policy | What happens |
|---|---|
| Auto-accept | The room accepts if it's free and declines if it's busy |
| Manual | The booking waits for the room's manager to approve it |
| Restricted to groups | Only members of certain groups can book it |

A room can't be double-booked: two people booking the same slot at the same moment can't both get it.
Rooms are booked for timed events only.
