**What you'll learn:** how conversations work, how to act on one or many at once, and how to use labels.

## Conversations

Mail groups replies into conversations (threads). The list shows one row per conversation with the senders,
a count such as "(3)", the subject, a snippet and the time. Unread conversations are bold with a dot at the left.

When you open a conversation, older messages you've read are collapsed to one line. The newest and any unread
messages are open. Select a collapsed message to expand it.

Opening a conversation marks it read after one second. Change this in **Settings → General → Mark as read**:
**Immediately**, **After 1 second** or **Manually**.

## Act on conversations

Hover over a row to see quick actions, or right-click it for the full menu. The right-click menu has:

- **Reply**, **Reply all**, **Forward** (one conversation only)
- **Archive**, **Delete**, **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**, **Star**
- **Move to** a folder or label, **Label as** with a checkbox per label and **New label…**
- **Block** the sender and **Report spam**

**Archive** removes a conversation from the Inbox but keeps it in **All mail** and under its labels.
**Delete** moves it to Trash.

Every action happens right away and shows a toast with **Undo**. Press `z` to undo the last action.

### Select several

Select the checkbox on each row, press `x` on the row under the keyboard cursor, or press Space on a focused row.
A bar with bulk actions appears above the list. Press `Esc` to clear the selection.

### Touch screens

On a phone or tablet, swipe a row left or right. By default, swiping right archives and swiping left marks as read.
Change both in **Settings → General → Swipe right / Swipe left** (Archive, Delete, Mark as read or Nothing).

## Labels

Labels work like folders, except a conversation can have more than one. Your labels appear in the sidebar with
a colored dot and an unread count.

<Steps>

1. Open the command palette (`Ctrl+K`) and choose **New label**, or use **New label…** in a row's
   **Label as** menu.

2. Enter a name and pick a color.

3. To nest it, choose a parent in **Nest under**. Don't type `/` in the name; use **Nest under** instead.

</Steps>

To label conversations, press `l` (label as) or `v` (move: add the label and remove it from the Inbox).
A picker opens with a search box and a checkbox per label. Press Space to toggle and `Ctrl+Enter` to apply.
You can also drag a conversation onto a label in the sidebar.

Edit, add a sublabel to, or delete a label in **Settings → Labels**, or right-click it in the sidebar.
Deleting a label doesn't delete the mail under it.

## Inbox categories

Turn on **Settings → General → Inbox categories** to split the Inbox into **Primary**, **Updates** and
**Promotions** tabs. Categories are backed by your labels. **Updates** uses a label named "Updates" or
"Notifications"; **Promotions** uses one named "Promotions", "Newsletters" or "Offers". A filter that applies
such a label sorts mail into that tab. **Primary** is the rest of the Inbox.

## Spam and Trash

Open Spam or Trash and select **Empty now** to delete everything in it permanently. You're asked to confirm.

To rescue a message from Spam, open it and select **Not spam**, or right-click it and choose **Not spam**.

<Aside type="note" title="Snooze and Mute">
Snooze and Mute are in the menus, but the server doesn't support them yet.
</Aside>
