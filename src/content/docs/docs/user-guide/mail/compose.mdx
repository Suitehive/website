**What you'll learn:** how compose windows work, how drafts are saved, and how to take back a message you just sent.

## Write a message

Press `c` or select **Compose**. A compose window opens at the bottom right. You can keep up to three windows
open side by side; older ones become minimized tabs. Each window has **Minimize**, **Full screen**
(press `Esc` to leave it) and **Save & close**.

Replies and forwards open inline, below the conversation. Press `r` to reply, `a` to reply all and `f` to forward.

Send with **Send** or `Ctrl+Enter` (`⌘Enter` on a Mac).

## Recipients

Type in **To** and pick from the suggestions with the arrow keys and `Enter`. Suggestions come from your
organization's directory and people you've emailed. Each recipient becomes a chip.

- Press `Enter`, `Tab`, `,` or `;` to turn what you typed into a chip. You can paste many addresses at once.
- Addresses outside your organization get an **external** tag, so you notice before you send.
- Groups show their member count.
- An invalid address turns red. You can't send until you fix or remove it.
- Add **Cc** and **Bcc** from the links next to **To**.

## Attachments

Drag files anywhere onto the compose window, or use **Attach files** or **Attach images**. Each file shows its
upload progress. **Send** waits until every upload is done and says **Uploading…** in the meantime.

If your message mentions "attached", "attachment" or "enclosed" but nothing is attached, Mail asks
**Send without an attachment?** Select **Keep editing** or **Send anyway**.

## Drafts

Drafts save automatically 1.5 seconds after you stop typing. The window shows **Saving…** and then **Saved**.
If you close the browser tab, unsaved windows get one last save. Find your drafts in the **Drafts** folder.

If you edit the same draft on two devices, Mail notices and offers **Keep this version** or **Load other**.

To throw a draft away, select **Discard draft** (the trash icon).

## Undo send

After you send, a toast says **Message sent** with **Undo** and **View**. Select **Undo** within the undo
window to stop the message and reopen it exactly as it was.

The window is 10 seconds by default. Change it in **Settings → General → Undo send**: Off, 5, 10, 20 or
30 seconds. After the window closes, the message is on its way and can't be taken back.

## Signatures

Set your signature in **Settings → Signatures**. Choose whether to add it to new messages, to replies and
forwards, or both. In a compose window, hover over the signature and select **Remove signature** to leave it
out of one message.

<Aside type="note" title="Sending from other addresses">
If your admin gave you other addresses to send from, a **From** field appears in compose.
</Aside>
