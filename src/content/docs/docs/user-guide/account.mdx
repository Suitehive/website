**What you'll learn:** what each section of your account does, how to set up 2-step verification, and how to
sign out a lost device.

Open your account from the account menu (your picture or initials at the top right) or go to
`https://suite.example.com/account`. One account covers Mail, Calendar and every other Suitehive app.

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| Profile | Name, language, time zone, date format, time format, photo |
| Security | Password, authenticator app, recovery codes, passkeys |
| Devices | Where you're signed in |
| App passwords | Passwords for mail and calendar apps |
| Connected apps | Other apps you allowed to use your account |
| Appearance | Theme, density, reading font |

## Profile

Change your **Name**, **Language** and **Time zone**. **Date format** and **Time format** follow your
language by default; you can pick a fixed style such as `2026-03-14` or `15:30` instead. Calendar uses your
time zone from here.

<Aside type="note" title="Profile photos">
The photo upload is in the page, but the server doesn't accept photos yet.
</Aside>

## Password

In **Security**, select **Change password**. Enter your current password and a new one of at least 12
characters (your organization may require more). A meter shows how strong it is. Passwords found in known
data breaches are refused.

Changing your password keeps you signed in on this device and signs you out everywhere else.

## 2-step verification

2-step verification asks for a second proof after your password. Your organization may require it.
You can use either method, or both.

### Authenticator app

<Steps>

1. In **Security**, select **Set up** next to **Authenticator app**.

2. Scan the QR code with an app such as Aegis, 1Password or Google Authenticator, or type the key shown below it.

3. Enter the 6-digit code from the app.

</Steps>

### Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, face or device screen lock. Under **Passkeys**, select **Add a passkey** and
follow your browser's prompt. Give each passkey a name you'll recognize; you can **Rename** or **Remove** it later.
When you sign in, the passkey prompt starts on its own if you have one.

### Recovery codes

When you turn on your first method, you get a set of recovery codes. Each one works once, if you lose your
phone or passkey. **Download** or **Copy** them and confirm **I saved my recovery codes**.

**Security** shows how many codes you have left. Select **Get new codes** for a fresh set; the old ones stop working.

<Aside type="caution" title="Sensitive changes ask again">
Changing your password or 2-step methods, or creating an app password, may ask you to confirm with a passkey or
code first, even though you're signed in. After you confirm, the change goes through.
</Aside>

## Devices

**Devices** lists every active session: browser and device, approximate location, IP address and when it was
last active. Your current one is marked **This device**.

- Select **Sign out** next to a session to end it.
- Select **Sign out all others** to end every session except this one.

If you lose a laptop or phone, sign it out here and change your password.

## App passwords

Mail apps and calendar apps (Thunderbird, Apple Mail, phone apps) don't sign in with your account password.
Each one gets its own app password.

<Steps>

1. Go to **App passwords** and select **Create app password**.

2. Give it a **Name** you'll recognize, such as "Thunderbird on laptop".

3. Under **Works with**, choose **Mail apps** (IMAP and SMTP), **Calendar apps** (CalDAV), or both.

4. Select **Create**. Copy the password now: it's shown once and can't be shown again.

</Steps>

The list shows when each password was created and last used. Select **Revoke** to turn one off; apps using it
stop syncing until you give them a new one. If your organization turned app passwords off, the page says so.

For server settings, see [Connecting apps](/docs/user-guide/connecting-apps/).

## Connected apps

Other tools that use "Sign in with Suitehive" appear here after you allow them. Select **Revoke access…** to
remove one.

## Appearance

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Theme | System, Light, Dark |
| Density | Comfortable, Compact (fits more rows in lists and tables) |
| Reading font | Sans or Serif, for email bodies |

Theme and density are also in the account menu.
