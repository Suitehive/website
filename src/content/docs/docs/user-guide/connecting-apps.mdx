**What you'll learn:** the server settings for mail and calendar apps, and how to add your account to the
most common ones.

## Before you start

Create an app password in **Account → App passwords**. Choose **Mail apps** for IMAP and SMTP, **Calendar apps**
for CalDAV, or both. Your account password doesn't work in these apps. See
[Your account](/docs/user-guide/account/).

## Server settings

Replace `suite.example.com` with your Suitehive hostname and `priya@example.com` with your email address.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Username | Your full email address, for example `priya@example.com` |
| Password | Your app password |
| Incoming mail (IMAP) | `suite.example.com`, port `993`, SSL/TLS |
| Outgoing mail (SMTP) | `suite.example.com`, port `587`, STARTTLS |
| Outgoing mail, alternative | `suite.example.com`, port `465`, SSL/TLS |
| Authentication | Normal password |
| CalDAV server | `https://suite.example.com/dav/` |

Mail shows your server name and ports, with copy buttons, in **Mail → Settings → Mail apps**. Calendar shows your exact
CalDAV address in **Calendar → Settings → Calendar apps**.

<Aside type="note" title="App password dialog">
After you create an app password, the dialog lists your username and may list server settings. Filling in the
server settings there is still being finished; until then, use the values on this page or in **Mail apps**.
</Aside>

### Local trial

In local mode the ports are different and the certificate is self-signed, so your app will ask you to trust it.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| IMAP | `localhost`, port `1993`, SSL/TLS |
| SMTP | `localhost`, port `2587`, STARTTLS, or port `2465`, SSL/TLS |
| CalDAV | `http://localhost:8080/dav/` |

## Mail apps

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Thunderbird">

<Steps>

1. Open **Account Settings → Account Actions → Add Mail Account**.

2. Enter your name, email address and app password, then select **Configure manually**.

3. Set incoming to IMAP, `suite.example.com`, port 993, SSL/TLS. Set outgoing to SMTP, `suite.example.com`,
   port 587, STARTTLS. Use **Normal password** and your full email address as the username for both.

4. Select **Done**.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Apple Mail (Mac)">

<Steps>

1. Open **Mail → Add Account**, choose **Other Mail Account**, and continue.

2. Enter your name, email address and app password, then select **Sign In**.

3. When Mail asks for server details, choose **IMAP** and enter `suite.example.com` for both the incoming and
   outgoing server, with your full email address as the username.

</Steps>

Apple Mail picks the port and security on its own. If sending fails, set the outgoing server to port 587 with
TLS in the account's **Server Settings**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings → Mail → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Mail Account**.

2. Enter your name, email address and app password, then select **Next**.

3. Choose **IMAP**. Enter `suite.example.com` as the host name for both incoming and outgoing mail, with your
   full email address and app password for both.

4. Select **Next** and **Save**.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Android">

Use any mail app that supports IMAP, such as Thunderbird for Android.

<Steps>

1. Add an account and choose manual setup, or IMAP, when asked.

2. Incoming: IMAP, `suite.example.com`, port 993, SSL/TLS.

3. Outgoing: SMTP, `suite.example.com`, port 587, STARTTLS (or 465, SSL/TLS).

4. Use your full email address and your app password for both.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Menu names vary between app versions. If a step doesn't match, look for "manual setup" or "other account".

## Calendar apps

Calendar sync uses CalDAV. Your app sees every calendar you can read, and changes sync both ways.

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Apple Calendar and iPhone">

On a Mac, open **Calendar → Add Account → Other CalDAV Account**. On iPhone or iPad, open
**Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add CalDAV Account**.

Use **Manual** account type if asked, your full email address, your app password, and `suite.example.com`
as the server.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Thunderbird">

Open the Calendar tab, choose **New Calendar → On the Network**, and enter your email address and
`https://suite.example.com/dav/` as the location. Sign in with your app password when asked, then pick the
calendars to add.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Android">

Android has no built-in CalDAV support. Install DAVx⁵, add an account with **Login with URL and user name**,
and enter `https://suite.example.com/dav/`, your email address and your app password. Your calendars then
appear in the phone's calendar app.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Autodiscovery

If your admin added the SRV records from [DNS setup](/docs/getting-started/dns/), apps that support DNS
service discovery can find the servers from your email address alone. `https://suite.example.com/.well-known/caldav`
also points calendar apps to the right place. If discovery doesn't work in your app, enter the settings by hand.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| The app rejects your password | Use an app password, not your account password. Check that it has the right scope (Mail apps or Calendar apps) and wasn't revoked |
| You can't create an app password | Your organization may have turned app passwords off. Ask your admin |
| Sending fails but receiving works | Try port 465 with SSL/TLS instead of 587 |
| A certificate warning in the local trial | Expected: the local trial uses a self-signed certificate |
