**What you'll learn:** what each organization security policy does, its default and allowed range, and how it is enforced.

Open **Admin**, then **Access › Security policies** (`/admin/security`). Changing policies needs the `auth.org.manage` permission. Every change is recorded in the [audit log](/docs/admin/audit-log/) as `auth.org.policy.update`.

## Policy reference

| Setting | Options | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Require 2-step** | Optional, Required for admins, Required for everyone | Required for admins | See [2-step verification](#2-step-verification) |
| **Enforce from** | A date | none | Saved, but not applied yet (see below) |
| **Minimum length** | 12 to 128 characters | 12 | Applies whenever a password is set |
| **Allow app passwords** | On or off | On | See [App passwords](#app-passwords) |
| **Session length** | 1, 7, 14, 30 or 90 days | 14 days | Maximum age of a sign-in |
| **Idle timeout** | 1, 8 or 24 hours, or 7, 14 or 30 days | 3 days | Signs out after this much inactivity. Can't exceed the session length. |
| **Sign in with** Google, Microsoft | On or off | Off | See [External sign-in](/docs/admin/external-sign-in/) |
| **Keep deleted accounts for** | 7, 14, 30, 60 or 90 days in the console (the API allows 1 to 365) | 30 days | How long a deleted user's address and data are kept before they are purged. See [Users](/docs/admin/users/). |
| **Mail to suspended people** | Keep receiving it, Bounce it | Keep receiving it | What Mail does with mail sent to a suspended user |

<Aside type="caution" title="Minimum length below 12">
The console's hint says 8 to 128 characters, but the server rejects values below 12. Use 12 or more.
</Aside>

## 2-step verification

People enroll an authenticator app (TOTP) or a passkey, and get recovery codes. The policy decides who must have one:

- **Optional:** nobody is forced. Anyone who has enrolled is still asked for their second step at sign-in.
- **Required for admins:** applies to the organization owner and anyone with a built-in admin role (`owner`, `admin`, `user_admin`, `helpdesk`). Custom roles don't count.
- **Required for everyone:** applies to every member.

When a person who must use 2-step signs in without one, they get a limited session that only lets them set it up, at `/account/security/setup`. They can't open Mail, Calendar or Admin until they finish. People can't remove their last 2-step method while the policy requires one. Invitations enforce the same rule.

The first admin created by `suitehive install` falls under the default policy, so you set up 2-step on your first sign-in.

<Aside type="note" title="Not available yet: a grace period">
**Enforce from** is stored, but sign-in doesn't read it yet: a requirement applies from each person's next sign-in after you save it. Tell people before you turn it on.
</Aside>

## Passwords

Suitehive follows current NIST guidance: there are no composition rules (no forced symbols or digits). Besides the minimum length, every password:

- has at most 256 characters;
- isn't blank, a single repeated character, or contains control characters;
- doesn't contain the person's email name, the organization name or a word of 4 or more letters from their name.

After 10 failed sign-ins an account is locked for 15 minutes. This isn't configurable.

<Aside type="note" title="Not available yet: breached-password check">
Checking passwords against known breaches is designed but not connected yet.
</Aside>

## Sessions

Session length and idle timeout are checked on every request against the current policy. Shortening the session length also ends existing sessions that are now too old.

## App passwords

Desktop and phone apps (IMAP, SMTP, CalDAV) sign in with app passwords that people create under **Account › App passwords** (up to 50 each). See [Connecting apps](/docs/user-guide/connecting-apps/).

When you turn **Allow app passwords** off:

- nobody can create new ones;
- existing app passwords stop working at once (they aren't deleted, and work again if you turn the setting back on).

Personal access tokens have no on/off policy.
