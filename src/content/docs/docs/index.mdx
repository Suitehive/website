Suitehive is an open-source, self-hostable alternative to Google Workspace: Mail, Calendar and single
sign-on for your team, on your own domain and your own server.

<Aside type="caution" title="Early development">
Suitehive is not production-ready yet. Container images haven't been published, so the installer needs
`--build` (build from source) or `--dry-run` until the first release.
</Aside>
